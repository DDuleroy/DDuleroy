## I'm David Duleroy, nice to meet U!


I work at the intersection of AI, creative systems, publishing, human–AI interaction and experimental software design.

My main focus is on tools and methods that help people preserve intent, context and continuity while working across different AI systems..


## Current work

🧬 **Genome**
An inter-provider continuity module designed to help users transfer, preserve and resume AI conversations across different models and environments with the idea of managing cogintive state.

🌐 **FlyIA**
An experimental environment for multi-AI workflows, cross-provider interaction, continuity, user control and structured collaboration between humans and AI systems.

🧠 **AI continuity & structured intent**
Research and prototyping around portable context, memory, handoff, conversation state, human validation and long-running AI-assisted work.

🛠️ **AI tooling & workflows**
Exploring browser extensions, agentic workflows, runtime orchestration and practical interfaces for working with multiple AI providers.

🎨 **Creative technology**
Applying AI to visual design, publishing, concept development, writing, creative workflows and experimental forms of human–machine collaboration.
